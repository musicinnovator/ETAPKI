Historical foundation

ETAPKI begins with taste.

ETAPKI is named for Essay on the True Art of Playing Keyboard Instruments, by Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach, son of Johann Sebastian Bach.

1753

Part One

Published in Berlin. It includes the practical discussion of Manieren - ornaments or embellishments - and performance.

1762

Part Two

Published in Berlin. It addresses accompaniment and free fantasia.

Von den Manieren

The German chapter title

The original table of contents calls Chapter Two “Von den Manieren”; the 1949 Mitchell translation presents it as “Embellishments.”

“the good must be correctly performed, and introduced moderately and fittingly.”C. P. E. Bach, Chapter Two, §5, Mitchell translation, p. 79.
“a prodigal use of embellishments must be avoided.”C. P. E. Bach, Chapter Two, §9, Mitchell translation, p. 81.

Short quotations are included for historical context. The English wording is from the supplied 1949 W. W. Norton edition; publication dates and the German chapter title are cross-checked against the historical text listing.
